Final record · Joshua 8:1
After the hidden sin was dealt with, God told Joshua, “Do not be afraid or discouraged.” The story does not end at Ai. God gives Joshua direction to move forward.

Take it beyond the game

Israel’s story in Joshua 7 is a specific moment in Israel’s history. It is not a formula that says every hard thing in life is caused by hidden sin. But it does remind us that hiding sin is not the same as dealing with it.

What would it look like to bring something you have been hiding into the light—with God, and with a trusted, wise person if you need help?
